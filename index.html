01 / Where is water found?

Fresh water vs. salt water

Water can be classified by how much dissolved salt it contains.

💦 Fresh water

Contains very little salt. We find it in rivers, lakes, glaciers and groundwater. It is essential for drinking, plants and animals.

🌊 Salt water

Contains large amounts of dissolved salts. We find it in oceans and seas. Most of Earth's water is salty.

97% of Earth's water is salt water; only about 3% is fresh water.

02 / Nature's recycling system

The water cycle 🔄

Water continuously moves between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.

☀️Evaporation

Sunlight heats liquid water, turning it into water vapor.

☁️Condensation

Water vapor cools and forms tiny droplets in clouds.

🌧️Precipitation

Water falls as rain, snow, sleet or hail.

🏞️Collection

Water gathers in rivers, lakes, oceans and the ground.

🌿 Plants also release water vapor through transpiration. Then the cycle repeats!

03 / Unusual precipitation

What is VIRGA? ☁️

Virga is rain or snow that falls from a cloud but evaporates or sublimates before reaching the ground.

🌤️ Why does it happen?

The air below the cloud is often very dry. Falling drops or ice crystals disappear as they pass through it.

👀 From far away, virga looks like thin, wispy streaks below a cloud.

04 / Powerful storms

How does hail form? 🧊

Hail forms inside strong thunderstorms called cumulonimbus clouds.

💧1. Water drops

Small drops are carried into a storm cloud.

⬆️2. Updrafts

Strong rising winds lift drops into freezing air.

❄️3. Ice layers

The drops freeze and grow as more water freezes onto them.

⬇️4. Hail falls

Hailstones fall when the updraft can no longer hold them.

🔍 Some hailstones have visible layers of ice, like an onion!

05 / Make a difference

How can we save water? 🌱

Fresh water is precious, and our daily choices can protect it.

🚰 Turn off taps while brushing🚿 Take shorter showers🔧 Repair leaking pipes🌧️ Use collected rainwater for plants🗑️ Keep rivers and lakes clean🧺 Wash full loads of clothes

💙 Saving water also saves energy used to treat and transport it.
